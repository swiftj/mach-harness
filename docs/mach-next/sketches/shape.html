IDEA-019 · revision 3 · edited today

Let members renew a loan themselves

Problem

A member who wants more time with a book has to call or come in. The desk spends hours a week on renewals that need no judgement, and members find out a renewal was refused only when a fine arrives.

Who has it

Members with items on loan, most of all those who cannot reach a branch in opening hours. Desk staff at Central, who take most of the calls.

Outcomes

  • O-1

    Members renew without calling the library.

    How we would know: renewal calls at Central fall from about 40 a week to under 15 within two months of release.

  • O-2

    Members are not surprised by a renewal that was refused.

    How we would know: not written yet

    O-2 has no way to know it happened. Say what you would see or count.

Not doing

  • Changing how many times a loan can be renewed.
  • Paying fines online (its own idea, decided Go).
  • Renewing by phone without a person.

Assumptions

  • A-1

    Most renewal calls are for items nobody is waiting for.

    Assumed, not confirmed
  • A-2

    Members who renew already sign in to the catalogue.

    Confirmed by the head of lending services
  • A-3

    Two renewals is still the right limit when members do it themselves.

    Assumed, not confirmed

Open questions

  • What does a member see when a renewal is refused because someone else is waiting? Who can answer: the head of lending services.

Risks

  • A branch loses sight of items it wanted back for a display.
  • Members renew on the last day and still return late, so fines do not fall.

The case

Before any epic exists: the case as a member would hear it, the hardest questions MACH could find, and your verdict.

As a member would hear it

You can now renew a loan from your account, at any hour, without calling us. If you cannot renew, the page tells you why straight away: the item is overdue, or someone else is waiting for it. Every renewal still follows the same rules the desk follows. Staff can see every renewal you make.

The hard questions

  1. MACH asked

    Who would not want this?

    Desk staff at the small branches, who use renewal calls to check on older members. Phone renewals stay; this is an extra way, not a replacement.

    Your answer
  2. MACH asked, because A-1 is not confirmed

    What does it cost if most calls turn out to be about held items (A-1 is wrong)?

    Calls fall less than we hope, and O-1 is not met. We would still have the clearer refusals of O-2.

    Your answer
  3. MACH asked

    What is the smallest version worth shipping?

    Renew one item from the loans list, with the reason shown when it is refused.

    Your answer
  4. MACH asked

    What would make you stop?

    Not answered yet Answer it
