Renew a loan

A patron extends a loan from their account page instead of asking at the desk.

What must always be true

  • INV-01A loan MUST NOT be renewed more than two times.
  • INV-02An overdue loan MUST NOT be renewed.

What it does

  • FR-01When a patron renews a loan, the system shall set the new due date to 21 days after the current due date.
  • FR-02If the loan has been renewed twice, then the system shall refuse and say “This loan cannot be renewed again.”
  • FR-03If the loan is overdue, then the system shall refuse and say “Return this item or pay the fine before renewing.”
  • FR-04A hold by another patron: being decided now
